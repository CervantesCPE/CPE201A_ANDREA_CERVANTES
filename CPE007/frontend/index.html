    ABOUT THE PROJECT
    BANTAY BAHA WARNING SYSTEM

    Building a more informed and flood-ready NCR.

    BaBaW is a location-aware flood information platform designed to help communities access useful weather information, explore geographic locations, and find reliable government resources in one convenient place.

    ⌖
    ◎

    Our Purpose

    BaBaW (Bantay Baha Warning System) is an academic project developed to provide residents of the National Capital Region with an accessible and user-friendly flood information dashboard.

    The platform combines location-based weather forecasts, an interactive NCR map, and links to official government flood and disaster preparedness resources.

    ◇

    Our Mission

    Our mission is to improve access to flood-related information by presenting relevant resources through a simple, organized, and understandable interface.

    Through this platform, we aim to encourage greater awareness, preparedness, and responsible decision-making among communities vulnerable to flooding.

    ⓘ

    Important Information

    BaBaW is an academic prototype and is not an official flood warning or emergency response service.

    The platform is not affiliated with PAGASA, NDRRMC, or any other government agency. For official warnings, evacuation announcements, and emergency instructions, always follow authorized government sources and your local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office.
